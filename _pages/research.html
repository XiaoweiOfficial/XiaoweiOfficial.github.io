My research examines how digital platforms and the firms that build on them create value together. I study platform owners' entry into complementary product markets, how complementors respond, and how category-level conditions shape both. My main empirical setting is the U.S. console video game industry; I am extending this work to intelligent and connected vehicles and other platform ecosystems.

For published work, see my publications. Full details are in my CV.
