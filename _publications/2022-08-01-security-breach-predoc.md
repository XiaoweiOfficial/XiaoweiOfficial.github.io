excerpt: "Security breaches can expose sensitive data and provoke sharp consumer reactions, yet how organizations should respond, and how consumers appraise those responses, has remained underspecified. Grounding a conceptual model in risk theory and protection motivation theory, we treat the breached organization as an agent offering a coping strategy and consumers as principals evaluating it. A vignette-based survey shows that variation in an organization's response strategy produces significantly different consumer reactions. <br /><br /><i>My contribution: synthesizing the literature, developing hypotheses, and building out the theoretical framework. Written during my Research Master, as a research assistant to Professor Carol Ou in the Information Systems department at Tilburg University.</i>"
