title: "Match, Simplify, or Expand: Salient Market Events and Subsequent Entrepreneurial Entries in Differentiated Product Spaces"
collection: research
type: "Working Paper"
excerpt: "Salient market events — products whose performance attracts widespread attention — are a recognized source of information for identifying entrepreneurial opportunities. But existing work examines entry within the same category, treating market structure as setting rather than explanation. We distinguish events by nature (exceptional success, technology failure, customer failure) and distinguish the product spaces entrants move into: a new product may *match* the category configuration of the event product, *expand* it by adding categories, or *simplify* it by retaining only some. Combining the discovery view of opportunities with prototype and goal-based views of categorization, we test the argument on video games, matching salient events on Steam to entrepreneurial entries on Kickstarter — 36 product spaces across 46 quarters (2014–2025), 138 events and over 10,000 entries. <br /><br /><i>With Jiahe Wang and Xiaofei Qu.</i>"
