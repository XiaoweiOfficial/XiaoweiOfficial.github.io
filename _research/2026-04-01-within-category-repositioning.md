title: "Within-Category Repositioning Given Benchmark Multiplicity"

excerpt: "How do established firms reposition when a category contains more than one benchmark? We examine two: the *prototype*, the central tendency of comparable products, and *exemplars*, concrete and favorably evaluated products. Greater distance between a firm's established portfolio and the prototype creates a larger discrepancy from prevailing category configurations, and is associated with both a greater departure from the firm's own recent portfolio and a stronger prototype-facing direction of change. These associations weaken when exemplars are prevalent, because a richer set of concrete, well-regarded configurations makes the prototype less exclusive as a reference. Tested on the U.S. console video game industry, 1995–2005, using embedding-based measures of publisher portfolios built from MobyGames descriptions. <br /><br /><i>With Xavier Martin and Niels Noorderhaven. Dissertation chapter. Targeted at Organization Science.</i>"
