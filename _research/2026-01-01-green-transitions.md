title: "Organizing Routines and Capabilities for Green Transitions"

excerpt: "Industry-wide transitions such as the shift toward net zero require large-scale organizational change, and technological innovation is usually central to a firm's response. Firms often try to reshape what members understand as strategically important and legitimate — what we call *shared cognition reframing*. Whether that alone produces technological innovation is unclear: many transition efforts are absorbed into existing ways of operating. We argue that cognition sets the direction of technological search while recurrent activity supplies its content, so reframing matters for innovation mainly where *routine reconfiguration* also occurs. Using large language models on 5,329 corporate sustainability reports, we build separate firm-year measures of each and relate them to subsequent patenting in the Y02 climate-mitigation classification, across 578 U.S. firms and 4,645 firm-years. <br /><br /><i>With Qinli Lu and Michael Park.</i>"
