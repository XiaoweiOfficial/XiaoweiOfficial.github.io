title: "Category Conditions and Strategic Entry: Unveiling the Motives for Platform Owner Entry"

type: "Working Paper"
excerpt: "Prior work on platform ecosystems largely treats first-party complements as substitutes for third-party ones, emphasizing value capture and competitive displacement. We develop a complementary view in which first-party complement provision can orchestrate the complementor ecosystem. Because first-party development capacity is scarce, platform owners deploy it selectively toward categories where its marginal ecosystem value is greater. On the supply side, it generates and demonstrates platform-specific development knowledge, which matters more where technical requirements are demanding and less where such knowledge has already been revealed. On the demand side, it supplies visible, platform-specific content where a category's uniqueness or commercial performance is weak. Tested on the U.S. console video game industry, 1995–2005, using hazard models with instrumental-variable robustness checks. <br /><br /><i>With Xavier Martin and Niels Noorderhaven. Dissertation chapter. Best Paper Proceeding, Academy of Management Annual Meeting 2025. Targeted at Strategic Management Journal.</i>"
